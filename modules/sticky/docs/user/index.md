# Sticky

Keeps a message at the bottom of a channel or thread, so new messages never bury it.

Whenever someone posts, the sticky is deleted and posted again below the new message. Moderators manage stickies with `/sticky` and need the Manage Messages permission by default.

> [!section] [Open](create.md)
> **`/sticky create`**
> Creates a sticky from a template and posts it right away.

> [!section] [Open](edit.md)
> **`/sticky edit`**
> Changes a sticky or switches it to another template.

> [!section] [Open](manage.md)
> **`/sticky show`, `/sticky list`, `/sticky delete`**
> Look at the stickies of your server or remove one.

> [!section] [Open](options.md)
> **Options**
> Ignoring bots, silent posts and waiting before a repost.
