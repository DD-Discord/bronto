# Mediaonly

Removes posts without an attachment or link from a channel, so it stays a channel for pictures, videos and other media.

Each channel or thread has its own rule. Moderators manage rules with `/mediaonly` and need the Manage Channels permission by default. Members with Manage Messages in a rule channel are exempt from its rule.

> [!section] [Open](setup.md)
> **`/mediaonly set`, `show`, `list`, `remove`**
> Create or change a rule, look at the rules of your server or remove one.
