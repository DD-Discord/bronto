# Forms

Forms that members fill in from a button, with the answers posted to a channel you choose.

Each form is a post in the forms forum. The post's first message shows the form's settings and fields, with buttons to change them. Members open the form from a button you put in a channel, answer in pop-up forms and get their submission posted for the team. Anyone who may post in the forum can create and change forms.

A new form is **inactive**: members cannot fill it in until you switch it on in **Settings**.

`/forms` needs Administrator by default. Building forms happens in the forum.

## Commands

- `/forms init` creates the forms forum, hidden from @everyone. It needs a Community server.
- `/forms list` lists the forms with links to their posts and whether they are active. Only you see the reply.
- `/forms post` puts a form button in a channel.
- `/forms buttons` lists the form buttons of a channel and removes them.
