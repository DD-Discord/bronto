# Autodelete

Keeps one post per member in a channel, enforces a cooldown between posts and removes a member's posts when they leave.

Each channel has its own rule. Moderators manage rules with `/autodelete` and need the Manage Messages permission by default. Members with Manage Messages in a rule channel are exempt from its rule.

> [!section] [Open](create.md)
> **`/autodelete create`**
> Creates a rule for a text or announcement channel.

> [!section] [Open](manage.md)
> **`/autodelete edit`, `show`, `list`, `delete`**
> Change a rule, look at the rules of your server or remove one.

> [!section] [Open](reset.md)
> **`/autodelete reset`**
> Lets a member post again before their cooldown ends.

> [!section] [Open](options.md)
> **Options**
> Keeping the latest post, cooldown, grace time, leaving members and direct messages.

> [!section] [Open](reports.md)
> **Reports and DMs**
> What the log channel and the direct messages show.
